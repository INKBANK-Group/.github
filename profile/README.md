# INKBANK

> Precision Chemistry. Jetting Mechanics. Digital Color Science.

**Shenzhen INKBANK New Materials Group Co., Ltd.** is a global developer and manufacturer of industrial digital inkjet inks and printhead-integrated solutions. Partnered with leading printhead OEMs and RIP vendors, we bridge advanced nanoscale chemistry with high-precision digital jetting.

---

### Capabilities & Engineering

* **Materials Science** — Nanoscale dispersion, ultra-pure dye/pigment synthesis, and UV-curable polymer formulations.
* **Waveform & Jetting Physics** — Rheology matching, voltage/pulse-width adaptation, and satellite droplet suppression for industrial printheads (Epson, Ricoh, Kyocera, Konica).
* **Color Management** — Substrate-specific ICC profiling, spectral color matching, and multi-channel linearization algorithms.
* **Intelligent Scale** — 40,000+ metric tons annual capacity across 50,000 m² of digitalized cleanroom facilities (Zhuhai Science Park).

---

### Core Domains

* **Textile & Apparel** — Sublimation, reactive, acid, and direct-to-film (DTF) pigment systems.
* **Industrial & Décor** — LED-UV curable formulations for architectural glass, ceramics, and rigid/flexible substrates.
* **Packaging & Commercial** — High-speed water-based pigment inks for corrugated, POD, and flexible substrates.
* **Functional Inks** — Conductive and specialty coatings for printed electronics and smart identification.
